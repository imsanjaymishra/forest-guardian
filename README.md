🌳 Forest Guardian

A 3D hunting-defense game built entirely in a single HTML file.
Protect your herd of goats, cows, sheep, and bedfellows from predators — tigers, lions, wild dogs, and hyenas — stalking in from the forest treeline. Take them down with your rifle before they reach the pasture and devour your livestock.

🎮 Play the Game
Option 1 — Play right now
Download forest-guardian.html
Open it in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox
Click ENTER FOREST and start playing

🎯 Gameplay
Wave-based combat — Survive 6 escalating waves of predators
Protect the herd — 12 livestock animals (goats, cows, sheep, bedfellows) graze in the pasture. If they all die, you lose
Predator AI — Tigers, lions, wild dogs, and hyenas spawn from the treeline, hunt the nearest livestock, and attack when close
Livestock AI — Animals wander the pasture and flee when predators approach
Scoring — Each predator killed earns points. Clear waves for bonus score
Health & Ammo — Manage your 8-round magazine and keep your vigilance bar up

🛠️ Tech Stack

Everything is procedural — no external assets:
3D models built from primitive geometries (boxes, cylinders, cones, spheres)
Gun viewmodel attached to camera with barrel pointing forward
Animals built part-by-part (body, head, legs, ears, eyes, tails, horns, stripes, manes)
Forest environment — trees, rocks, grass tufts, flowers, fence, barn, hay bales
Sound effects — gunshots, reloads, growls, cries, hit markers, bird chirps, parrot squawks, peacock calls, cuckoo calls
